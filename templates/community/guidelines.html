Stay relevant and respectful

Ask clear questions, explain your experience and discuss ideas without attacking people. Keep contributions relevant to the programme or article. Do not post harassment, spam, impersonation or material you do not have permission to share.

Protect personal information

Your username and approved messages are public. Use a public-facing username. Do not post passwords, application documents, private contact information or details about someone else without their permission. Use the contact form for personal support.

How moderation works

New topics and comments enter a review queue. Programme moderators can publish or hide contributions, lock discussions and mark issues resolved. A resolved status reflects the moderator’s handling of the topic, not a service guarantee. There is no guaranteed response time.

Reports and corrections

Use Report beside a published contribution to alert moderators. Reports are private to the moderation team. You can withdraw your own contributions from My activity. For account correction or deletion requests, contact the GECP team.

Issues and technical reports

Choose Issue when describing a website or programme problem. Explain what happened and what you expected, without including private data. Send security-sensitive details privately through the contact page, not in public threads.
